Epipremnum aureum

Pothos

$00

Pothos is the trailing plant that makes everyone look like they have a green thumb. Heart-shaped leaves cascade generously from shelves, mantels, and hanging vessels, softening a room with effortless, tumbling greenery. Fast-growing and endlessly forgiving, it is the perfect first plant or generous gift.

Pothos adapts to low or bright indirect light and prefers its soil to dry slightly between waterings. Trim it whenever you like — it only grows back fuller.
