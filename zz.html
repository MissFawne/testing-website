Zamioculcas zamiifolia

ZZ Plant

$00

The ZZ Plant is one of the most forgiving plants you can own — glossy, deep-green leaves on architectural upright stems that bring a calm, sculptural presence to any room. It asks for almost nothing and rewards you regardless, making it the ideal gift for the busy, the forgetful, or the self-described plant-killer.

The ZZ thrives in low to bright indirect light and prefers its soil to dry out almost completely between waterings. Overwatering is the only real way to harm it — when in doubt, wait.
