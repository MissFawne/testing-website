Alocasia × amazonica

Alocasia

$00

Alocasia is pure botanical sculpture — dramatic, arrow-shaped leaves with bold, pale veining set against deep, almost-black green. It is a true statement plant for the collector who wants something extraordinary, rewarding attentive care with breathtaking, architectural foliage.

Alocasia thrives in bright, indirect light with consistently moist (never soggy) soil and enjoys higher humidity. It is expressive about its needs and best suited to those who like to pay attention.
