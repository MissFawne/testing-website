Cordyline fruticosa ‘Red Sister’

Hawaiian Ti Plant

$00

Cordyline fruticosa ‘Red Sister’, commonly known as the Hawaiian Ti Plant, is a vibrant tropical plant prized for its bold, colourful foliage in shades of deep pink, red, and burgundy. Its upright growth and striking leaves make it a natural statement piece for patios, entryways, and bright indoor spaces. Whether used as a focal point in a container or to add a splash of tropical colour to your home, this plant brings warmth and personality wherever it grows.

This eye-catching plant thrives in bright, indirect light, prefers lightly moist soil with good drainage, and will benefit from occasional misting or higher humidity to keep its foliage looking lush and vibrant.
